Frequently asked questions

You are in Casablanca: who comes to our site in Tangier?

The metal furniture is made in our Tangier workshop. The sales office is in Casablanca. The area covered and the lead time for the on-site survey are confirmed when you first contact us.

Which products are made in Tangier?

Metal lockers, cabinets and pedestals. Seating, desks and material handling equipment are sourced from suppliers. Each product sheet states its origin.

How much does the on-site survey cost?

It is free. A sales rep records dimensions, access and your uses; OBM then proposes a 2D/3D plan and a quote.

How soon will I receive my quote?

Within 48 working hours, once your request is complete: quantities, plan or dimensions of the space, photos of the site.

Can you make custom furniture?

Yes, for products made in Tangier: non-standard dimensions and colours are assessed at quote stage.

What load per level for shelving?

It depends on the chosen configuration. Permissible loads are specified in the quote and the layout plan, after assessing your goods.

Do you deliver before the start of the school year?

The delivery schedule is set in the quote. For a school-year start, state your deadline in your request.

Are prices shown on the website?

No: each quote depends on quantities and configuration. It is prepared after a survey, with no obligation.
